# 🇵🇭 Kwiztorya

> **Learn • Play • Discover Philippine History**

Kwiztorya is an Android educational quiz game designed to help users learn Philippine history through fun and interactive gameplay. Players can answer quizzes, explore historical stories, unlock achievements, and improve their knowledge of the Philippines.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🧠 **Interactive Quiz** – Answer multiple-choice questions about Philippine history.
- 📖 **Story Mode** – Learn historical events through engaging stories.
- 📚 **Chapter Selection** – Explore different periods of Philippine history.
- 🏆 **Achievement System** – Unlock achievements as you progress.
- 📊 **Score Tracking** – Monitor your quiz performance.
- ⚙️ **Settings** – Customize your app experience.
- 🎮 **Easy-to-Use Interface** – Simple and beginner-friendly design.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

Kwiztorya was created to make learning Philippine history more enjoyable through educational quizzes and interactive storytelling. It provides an engaging way for students and history enthusiasts to discover important historical events, influential figures, and significant places while having fun.
